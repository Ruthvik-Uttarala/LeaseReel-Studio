# Editing Content

Most business copy lives in `src/content/site.ts`.

- Price: edit `site.price` and pricing CTA copy if the offer changes.
- Turnaround: edit `site.turnaround`, trust statements, and pricing delivery language.
- Emails: edit `site.emails.hello` and `site.emails.founder`.
- Founder copy: edit `site.founder`.
- FAQ: edit `site.faq`.
- Demos: edit `site.demo.tabs`, video paths, and set `site.demo.assetsReady` to `true` only after final media is added.
- CTA text: edit component labels in `Hero.tsx`, `PricingCard.tsx`, and `FinalCTA.tsx` if the offer changes.

Keep claims conservative. Do not add fake customers, fake logos, fake testimonials, or unverified leasing-performance claims.
